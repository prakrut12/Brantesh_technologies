Hard water hides in plain sight

Most of the damage happens inside your pipes, long before you notice it at the tap. Here is what to look out for.

  • Stained, smelly water

    Yellow-brown streaks in basins and buckets, and a metallic smell at the tap.

  • Sand and dust in the water

    Fine particles settle in tanks and clog taps, showers and pumps.

  • Blocked, narrowing pipes

    Scale and mud build up inside the pipe, so water pressure drops over time.

  • What you want instead

    Clear, fresh-feeling water that is kind to skin, clothes, appliances and plants.

Questions people ask us

How is this different from a salt-based softener?

Salt-based softeners swap calcium and magnesium for sodium and flush salty water down the drain. Brantesh conditioners use no salt and no chemicals, so nothing extra goes into your drinking water, plants or septic tank.

Does it remove the hardness minerals from the water?

A conditioner changes how the minerals behave so they stop forming scale. The minerals stay in the water, which is why drinking water keeps its natural content. If you want to discuss your specific water report, message us.

Will it clear scale that has already formed?

Yes. Our systems are designed to loosen existing scale in the plumbing and prevent new scale from forming.

How much water does it waste?

Very little. We estimate about 2% of the water is used up by the system. There is no regular flush of salty water like in salt-based units.

Do I need a filter as well?

It depends on your water. If you see sand, mud, colour or smell, a sand and carbon filter placed before the conditioner is a good idea. We will check your water and tell you honestly.
